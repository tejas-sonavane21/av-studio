The Aqualux difference

This is not a rental desk on the beach

Aqualux runs one shoreline - the one in front of Shreenika Coastal Retreat - and keeps the fleet small on purpose. Fewer machines, more time on the water for the people who came for the jetcars.

Jetcars are the headline

Three jet-propelled craft, 110 to 230 HP, maintained every morning before the first slot. Everything else on the menu is optional.

01

An instructor on every ride

First timers ride with a spotter behind them. Briefing, throttle, hand signals - all covered before you leave the sand.

02

The retreat, not just the sea

Showers, changing rooms, lockers, parking and a shaded deck at Shreenika Coastal Retreat. Reserve the deck and stay for the sunset.

03

Three windows, capped slots

Golden Hour, High Tide Day and the Sunset Session. We cap each window so the water in front of the retreat stays open.
